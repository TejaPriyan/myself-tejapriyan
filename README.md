# 🏝️ Glass-Tech Sanctuary 2026

> Ultimate fusion of medicine, engineering, AI and play — a personal portfolio & gaming hub by **Myself Teja Priyan**.

Glass-Tech Sanctuary is an interactive, full-stack digital universe that fuses personal portfolio showcases with multiple experiential zones: anatomy and medical study tools, engineering challenges, a full suite of mini-games, an interactive 3D model gallery, and **Ami** — an intelligent conversational companion with multiple operational modes.

> **🌐 Live Website:** [myself-tejapriyan.onrender.com](https://myself-tejapriyan.onrender.com/)

---

## ✨ What We Have in Glass-Tech Sanctuary

### 🩺 Medical Zone
* **Interactive 3D Human Brain (`human_brain.glb`)**: Full 3D orbital inspection of neuroanatomy, cerebral lobes, and high-yield physiology facts.
* **Beating Heart 3D Simulation (`beating-heart.glb`)**: Dynamic cardiac cycle visualization with rhythmic anatomical contraction.
* **Medical Study Utilities**: Speed quizzes, pharmacology drug-matching challenges, flashcard trainers, and clinical calculators.

### ⚙️ Engineer Zone
* **3D Cyberpunk Gadget Showcase**: Interactive inspection of high-fidelity 3D assets including the Cyberpunk Laptop (`cyberpunk_laptop.glb`), Drones, UFO (`ufo.glb`), and custom mechanical models.
* **Engineering Logic & Challenges**: Code puzzles, problem-solving trials, and technical simulations.

### 🎮 Game Zone (Mind & Skill Suite)
* **Playable Mini-Games**: Neural Memory, Speed Quiz, Stacker, Wordle, Drug Matcher, Typing Race, Minesweeper, and Flappy Bird.
* **Progression System**: Real-time XP gain, leveling, daily challenges, achievements, and persistent global leaderboards.

### 🤖 Ami AI Assistant (2026)
* **Conversational AI Partner**: Embedded AI with warm, concise, and adaptive intelligence across multiple modes:
  * **General Study & Assistance**: Medical, engineering, and programming Q&A.
  * **Quiz Generation**: On-the-fly interactive quizzes on any topic.
  * **Collaborative Story Mode**: Interactive storytelling where you and Ami build narratives together.
  * **Debate Mode**: Thoughtful counter-arguments and analytical discussions.
  * **Personality Analysis**: Behavioral insights based on interaction history.

### 🎨 Creative & Social Features
* **AI Image Generation**: Multi-provider generative image pipeline for high-resolution visual synthesis.
* **Real-time Global Chat**: Live community chat and presence powered by WebSockets.
* **Theme Customization**: Six bespoke aesthetic presets — *Cream, Dark, Ocean, Sunset, Neon, and Lavender*.
* **Progressive Web App (PWA)**: Installable on mobile and desktop with offline support.

## 🧰 Tech Stack

| Component | Technology |
|---|---|
| **Frontend** | HTML5, Modern CSS3 (Glassmorphism & Custom Theming), Vanilla JavaScript |
| **3D Graphics** | Three.js (r128) with GLTFLoader & OrbitControls |
| **Backend** | Node.js, Express, WebSocket Engine |
| **AI Intelligence** | Multi-model LLM integration & Generative Vision APIs |
| **PWA** | Service Worker with offline caching & Web App Manifest |
